# SOP: lockout/tagout (LOTO), all Corvane plants

Synthetic Corvane document. Applies to all maintenance work at IND1 and MTY1.

## 1. General rules

- Every technician working on the machine puts on a personal lock. One lock per person.
- Verify zero energy before starting work: try to start the machine from its controls.
- Hot surfaces: wait until surfaces are below 50 °C or wear heat-rated gloves.

## 2. Air compressors (CMP-01, CMP-02, CMP-03)

1. Stop the compressor from the controller and wait for the unload cycle to finish.
2. Open and lock the main disconnect. Tag it with name, date and work order number.
3. Close and lock the discharge isolation valve to the plant header.
4. Vent the receiver and separator tank through the blowdown valve until the gauge reads
   0 bar. Wait 5 minutes for the fluid to settle.
5. The airend and cooler can stay above 70 °C for 30 minutes after stopping. Let them cool
   before opening the cooler or the separator tank.
6. At MTY1, tell the machining hall supervisor before isolating CMP-03: it is the only
   compressor and the hall will stop.

## 3. CNC machining centers (CNC-01 to CNC-08)

1. Press emergency stop, then open and lock the main disconnect.
2. Close and lock the pneumatic supply valve and bleed the air through the dump valve.
3. The drawbar disc spring stack holds stored energy. Only release it with the drawbar
   press fixture (F-31).
4. Lock the coolant pump breaker separately; it is on its own circuit.

## 4. Conveyors and test stands

- Conveyors: lock the drive disconnect and the pull-cord reset.
- Test stands: lock the loop pump disconnect and drain the test loop to 0 bar before
  opening any pump seal.
