# Service notes: PTS-2 pump test stands (TST-01, TST-02, TST-03)

Synthetic Corvane document. The stands run acceptance tests on CP-100, CP-200 and CP-400 pumps.

- Loop pump mechanical seal: 35 mm, SKU SP-SEAL-0350. Leak rate warning 2 l/h, trip 5 l/h.
  Drain the loop to 0 bar before opening the seal (see the lockout/tagout SOP). Three hours.
- Flowmeter: deviation against the reference meter, warning 1.5 %, trip 3 %. Recalibrate on
  site (instrument technician, two hours). Test results from a stand with a drifting meter
  must be held by quality until the meter is recalibrated.
- Semi-annual calibration is a preventive work order in the CMMS.
