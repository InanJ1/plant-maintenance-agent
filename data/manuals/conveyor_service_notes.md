# Service notes: Arden RC-1200 roller conveyors (CNV-01, CNV-02, CNV-03)

Synthetic Corvane document.

- Roller bearings: 6205 sealed, Corvane SKU SP-BRG-0301. Replace in pairs (both ends of the
  roller). Three hours for two rollers.
- Vibration on the drive and tail sections: warning 6 mm/s, trip 10 mm/s. Grinding noise at
  the tail section usually means a roller bearing.
- Belt drift: warning 25 mm, trip 40 mm. Adjust the tracking idlers a quarter turn at a time
  and run for five minutes between adjustments. No parts needed.
- Quarterly inspection: belt tracking, roller condition, guards and pull-cord switches.
