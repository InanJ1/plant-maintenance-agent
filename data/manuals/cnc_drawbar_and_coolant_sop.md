# SOP: drawbar clamp force check and coolant filter change (CNC-01 to CNC-08)

Synthetic Corvane document.

## 1. Drawbar clamp force check

- Nominal clamp force 20 kN (VX-640) and 32 kN (HX-500).
- Minimum acceptable: 18 kN (VX-640), 28 kN (HX-500). Below 15 kN on a VX-640, stop the
  machine: tools can pull out during roughing.
- Check monthly with the clamp force gauge in the tool crib (gauge ID G-112).

Steps:
1. Load the clamp force gauge like a toolholder.
2. Clamp and unclamp three times. Record the third reading.
3. If the reading is under the minimum, replace the disc spring stack SP-SPRG-0031.

## 2. Disc spring stack replacement

- Part: SP-SPRG-0031. Time: 4 hours. Skills: spindle rebuild.
- The spring stack is preloaded. Use the drawbar press fixture (F-31). Never release the
  stack without the fixture: stored spring energy can eject parts.
- Stack the new springs in the same orientation as the old ones (alternating pairs).
- After assembly, verify clamp force with the gauge before releasing the machine.

## 3. Coolant filter change

- Each machine has one filter housing with two cartridges (SP-FLT-0310, 25 micron).
- Change both cartridges when filter differential pressure reaches 1.2 bar, or when coolant
  pressure at the spindle drops below 3.0 bar.
- Time: one hour. Skim tramp oil from the tank at the same time.
- If coolant pressure is low within 90 days of a filter change, the tank needs a full flush:
  log it on the work order so the planner can schedule it.
